### 1. Module Overview
#### 1.1 Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to introduce the fundamental concepts, technologies, applications, and ethical considerations of Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Through collecting, organizing, and explaining information from reliable sources, this module aims to make AI knowledge accessible to beginners.

Rather than focusing only on technical theories, the project connects AI concepts with familiar examples, practical activities, and real-world situations. Learners can gradually understand how AI works, where it is used, what benefits it offers, and what challenges it creates.

#### 1.2 Target Learners
This module is designed for:
- Learners: Students or beginners who are interested in Artificial Intelligence; learners exploring technology who do not have a background in programming or computer science.
- Current Knowledge Level: Have heard of or experienced familiar AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Gemini), but do not yet fully understand how AI works or its underlying nature.
- Learning Goals: Help learners master core knowledge, enabling them to effectively apply AI to their studies, work, and daily life.

#### 1.3 Learning Approach
The module follows a beginner-friendly learning approach:
  1. Understand: Learn fundamental AI concepts through simple explanations.
  2. Explore: Discover different AI technologies and their capabilities.
  3. Connect: Relate theoretical knowledge to familiar real-world applications.
  4. Experiment: Participate in practical activities and simple demonstrations.
  5. Evaluate: Think critically about AI's benefits, limitations and ethical issues.


**- The project's purpose and intended learners:** The purpose of this project is to explore, collect, and summarize information about AI in a clear and easy-to-understand way, making it accessible to beginners, learners and anyone who interested in AI.

## 2. What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science focused on building systems or machines capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence (such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and speech or image recognition).

### The Relationship among AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning:
We can visualize this relationship as a series of nested sets:

1. **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The overarching field — machines simulating intelligent human behavior.
2. **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI — machines learning patterns from data without being explicitly programmed with hard rules.
3. **Deep Learning (DL):** A subset of ML — utilizing multi-layered artificial neural networks to process complex data (images, audio, text).

| Criteria | Artificial Intelligence (AI) | Machine Learning (ML) | Deep Learning (DL) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | Broadest umbrella | Intermediate | Narrow & Specialized |
| **Working Principle** | Simulating intelligent behavior | Learning rules from data | Modeling biological neural networks |
| **Data Required** | Varies by approach | Moderate | Massive (Big Data) |

## 3. Practical Case Study
* **Scenario:** Face Recognition for unlocking an iPhone (Face ID) falls under which domain?
* **Answer & Explanation:** This is an application of **Deep Learning** within the field of **Computer Vision**, which is a specialized subset of Machine Learning and overall AI.
