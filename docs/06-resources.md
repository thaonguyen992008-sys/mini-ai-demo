# Learning Resources

## 1. Introduction to AI

Learn the basic concepts, definitions, and applications
of Artificial Intelligence.

- IBM: AI Guide
  https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-guide

## 2. AI Technologies

Explore Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and
Generative AI.

- IBM: Machine Learning
  https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/machine-learning

- IBM: Generative AI
  https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/generative-ai

## 3. AI Applications and Development

Explore AI research, industry applications, education,
healthcare, and economic impacts.

- Stanford HAI: AI Index Report
  https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report

## 4. AI Ethics and Responsibility

Learn about fairness, privacy, transparency,
human rights, and responsible AI.

- UNESCO: Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
  https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/recommendation

## 5. AI Risk Management

Understand how organizations can identify and manage
risks associated with AI systems.

- NIST: AI Risk Management Framework
  https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

## 6. Tips for Learning AI

- Start with basic concepts before studying advanced topics.
- Use reliable educational and academic sources.
- Compare information from multiple sources.
- Check publication dates when using statistics.
- Practice with AI tools to understand their capabilities
  and limitations.

## Conclusion

These resources provide a starting point for anyone
who wants to learn about AI, from basic concepts to
responsible AI development.
