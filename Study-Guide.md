### 1. Module Overview
#### 1.1 Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to introduce the fundamental concepts, technologies, applications, and ethical considerations of Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Through collecting, organizing, and explaining information from reliable sources, this module aims to make AI knowledge accessible to beginners.

Rather than focusing only on technical theories, the project connects AI concepts with familiar examples, practical activities, and real-world situations. Learners can gradually understand how AI works, where it is used, what benefits it offers, and what challenges it creates.

#### 1.2 Target Learners
This module is designed for:
- Learners: Students or beginners who are interested in Artificial Intelligence; learners exploring technology who do not have a background in programming or computer science.
- Current Knowledge Level: Have heard of or experienced familiar AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Gemini), but do not yet fully understand how AI works or its underlying nature.
- Learning Goals: Help learners master core knowledge, enabling them to effectively apply AI to their studies, work, and daily life.

#### 1.3 Learning Approach
The module follows a beginner-friendly learning approach:
  1. Understand: Learn fundamental AI concepts through simple explanations.
  2. Explore: Discover different AI technologies and their capabilities.
  3. Connect: Relate theoretical knowledge to familiar real-world applications.
  4. Experiment: Participate in practical activities and simple demonstrations.
  5. Evaluate: Think critically about AI's benefits, limitations and ethical issues.
